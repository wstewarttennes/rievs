How a full conversion works from out of state

  1. Conversation — by email, phone, or text: the vehicle, how you drive, what you want from it.
  2. Spec and quote — motor, pack size, range target, and a real parts list.
  3. Drop-off — you drive, trailer, or ship the vehicle to Rhode Island.
  4. Build — with photos as we go.
  5. Shakedown and pickup — commissioning, road testing, and a walkthrough of how to live with it.

New England conditions

Converting a vehicle here isn't the same as converting one in California.

  • Rust — decades of road salt. Frames and battery mounting points get inspected before anything else, and repairs happen before the pack goes in.
  • Cold — lithium cells shouldn't be charged below freezing. The BMS protects the pack during charging, and we talk through heating and where the vehicle will be parked.
  • Heat and defrost — a New England vehicle needs a real plan for cabin heat and a clear windshield, not an afterthought.
